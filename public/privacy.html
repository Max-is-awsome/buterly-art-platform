How Butterfly uses your data

Updated November 26, 2026.

You can explore without an account. To create, Butterfly uses Google sign-in and stores your Google account identifier and verified email address. Your email is not included in public artwork records. Butterfly does not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or calendar, and does not retain Google access or refresh tokens.

Your creations and private work

Published artwork, titles, descriptions, media, and inspiration connections are public. Do not include personal information you do not want other visitors to see. Drafts and bookmarks are associated with your account and require sign-in to access. Starting questions are public, and Butterfly keeps a private lifetime count of how many you have published to enforce the five-question limit.

An optional artist profile stores the name, bio, and any optional social links you enter. Butterfly shows this profile on artwork you choose to credit. Profile edits update those artworks, and clearing your profile removes the credit from public view.

Storage and cookies

Railway hosts the backend, PostgreSQL database, and backup storage. Vercel hosts the frontend and routes requests to the backend. Google handles sign-in. An essential session cookie keeps you signed in for up to seven days. Hosting services may process technical request logs, including IP addresses, to operate their services.

Retention and requests

Account records and work remain stored until removed by the operator. Database backups may retain earlier copies; backups currently have no automatic expiration. To request access, correction, or deletion of your data, contact the Butterfly project maintainer. Requests are handled manually. This is an early release; self-service account deletion is not yet available.

Google data

Google account data is used to authenticate you and associate your private work with your account. It is not sold or used for advertising. Butterfly does not use Google account data to train AI models.
